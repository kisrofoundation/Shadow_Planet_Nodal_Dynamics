Rahu and Ketu occupy an important position in ancient Indian astronomy, astrology and cosmological philosophy. They have been discussed for centuries in connection with eclipses, planetary movements, time cycles and the relationship between celestial events and human life.  In traditional Indian knowledge, Rahu and Ketu are known as Chhaya grah.
